# QUANTA V4 — Comprehensive Atomic Coverage Audit Matrix (All 118 Elements)

## Executive Summary

This document presents the exhaustive, element-by-element scientific and interactive coverage audit for **QUANTA V4**. Every chemical element from Hydrogen ($Z=1$) to Oganesson ($Z=118$) has been individually audited, upgraded, and verified against the **Hydrogen Reference Standard**.

### Audit Methodology & Verification Criteria
Each of the 118 elements is evaluated across six essential educational and scientific dimensions:
1. **Overview (O)**: Full physical profile, standard atomic mass, ground electronic configuration, spectroscopic ground term, first ionization energy, Pauling electronegativity, atomic radius, shell distribution, standard/predicted state, and discovery context.
2. **Shell Model (S)**: Pedagogical electron shell visualization with principal shell occupancies ($K$ through $Q$), core vs. valence differentiation, electron conservation ($N_e = Z$), and interactive state transitions.
3. **Quantum Mode (Q)**: Quantum probability density representation appropriate to the element's model tier, subshell breakdown ($s, p, d, f$), Slater screening constant $\sigma$, effective nuclear charge $Z_{\text{eff}}$, and Pauli/Hund's rule analysis.
4. **Energy Levels (E)**: Interactive visual energy ladder (Grotrian-style diagram) displaying ionization threshold, ground state, bound NIST ASD excited levels, term symbols, configurations, and $J$ values. For elements lacking laboratory bound levels, an explicitly attributed educational status is provided.
5. **Spectroscopy (Sp)**: Multi-band continuous electromagnetic spectrum strip spanning Ultraviolet ($<380\text{ nm}$), Visible ($380-740\text{ nm}$ with continuous optical dispersion), and Infrared ($>740\text{ nm}$), with landmark lines, derived vacuum and air wavelengths (Edlén index), transition wavenumber, and photon energy.
6. **Equations (Eq)**: 4-stage mathematical derivation inspector formatted with KaTeX:
   - **01 / Concept**: Physical principles of electron binding and photon absorption/emission.
   - **02 / Formulation**: Non-relativistic $Z$-electron Hamiltonian, Slater screening rule $Z_{\text{eff}} = Z - \sigma$, and photon energy relations $\Delta E = hc/\lambda$.
   - **03 / Substitution**: Parameter grid showing $Z$, electron count, valence subshell, calculated $\sigma$, and $Z_{\text{eff}}$.
   - **04 / Result**: Hero metrics for level separation $\Delta E$, $\lambda_{\text{vac}}$, wavenumber $\Delta\tilde{\nu}$, and estimated binding energy.

## Scientific Model Tier Classification

- **Tier A (Exact Analytic)**: Hydrogen ($Z=1$) — exact Schrödinger wavefunctions $|\psi_{nlm}|^2$ and analytical Rydberg eigenvalues.
- **Tier B (Dedicated Multi-Electron)**: Helium ($Z=2$) through Boron ($Z=5$) — specialized two-to-five electron Hamiltonians, spin-exchange splitting (Singlet/Triplet), and dedicated subshell clouds.
- **Tier C (Universal Semi-Empirical & Slater Screening)**: Carbon ($Z=6$) through Hassium ($Z=108$) — multi-electron non-relativistic Hamiltonians, Slater screening calculations ($Z_{\text{eff}} = Z - \sigma$), NIST ASD tabulated levels and derived spectroscopy.
- **Tier D (Synthetic / Unmeasured Optical Spectrum)**: Meitnerium ($Z=109$) through Oganesson ($Z=118$) — theoretical electronic structures, predicted ground terms, and explicit educational notices detailing the experimental limits of current laboratory spectroscopy without fabricated measurements.
